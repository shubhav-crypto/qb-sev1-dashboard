Workflow spotlight

Case Pulse

Streamlines CSM notifications with one context-rich workflow.

01

One-click CSM lookup

Fetches the CSM from Account Team Members and posts an internal Chatter comment, mentioning the CSM automatically.

02

No-response alert

Auto-generates a standard message with idle days, the last response date, and the upcoming closure date.

03

Custom message

A free-text editor lets engineers add a tailored note with supporting text, hyperlinks, and images.

04

Architect engagement

A dedicated notification format flags cases requiring architect-level involvement.

05

Smart OOO check

Detects CSM out-of-office status before posting, shows the return date, and lets the engineer cancel or proceed.

06

Manager always in loop

The case owner's manager is automatically copied as FYI on every Chatter post, with no manual tagging needed.
