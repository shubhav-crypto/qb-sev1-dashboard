01 · Root cause

The failure was architectural, not episodic

A full-history audit revealed that chats, cases, and leads were routed through one Omni-Channel queue, an unsupported design. A separate Case Step routing rule was also scoped outside its intended queue.

Chats→Shared queue
Cases→Shared queue
Leads→Shared queue

Structural correction

Each object→Dedicated queue

02 · Coordinated response

Support-led, not support-reactive

Unified ownership

Established a dedicated Support-Success channel, replacing fragmented case-by-case handling with one accountable operating rhythm.

Diagnosed the pattern

Audited the complete case history, isolated the unsupported queue design, and separated the routing defect from the scoping error.

Implemented a durable fix

Prescribed per-object queues aligned with documented routing architecture and corrected the Case Step configuration.

Protected customer trust

Corrected a dismissive support interaction and maintained precise Sev1 status: customer action versus engineering action.

Strengthened the Product signal

Validated the account's 18-month pattern analysis, added missing cases, and converted customer concerns into technically reviewed evidence.
